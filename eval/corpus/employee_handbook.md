# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees
may shift their day by up to two hours in either direction with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours, when everyone is expected to be reachable, are 11:00 AM
to 4:00 PM.

## Cafeteria

The cafeteria on the ground floor opens at 8:00 AM and closes at 7:30 PM on
weekdays. Breakfast is served until 10:30 AM. Lunch service runs from 12:00 PM
to 2:30 PM. The coffee bar next to the cafeteria stays open until 9:00 PM.

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued
monthly. Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried over to the next calendar
year. Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, and a medical certificate is
required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from both the direct manager and the department head.
All remote workers must be available during core collaboration hours.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days
of purchase. Individual expenses above 5,000 rupees require pre-approval from a
manager. Reimbursements are paid out with the next monthly payroll cycle.
