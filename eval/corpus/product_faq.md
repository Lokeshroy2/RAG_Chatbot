# CloudSync Product FAQ

## What is CloudSync?

CloudSync is a file synchronization service that keeps folders identical across
Windows, macOS, and Linux machines. Files are encrypted end-to-end before they
leave your device using AES-256 encryption.

## Pricing

The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage and syncs between 2 devices. The Pro
plan costs $8 per month, includes 2 TB of storage, unlimited devices, and
30-day file version history. The Team plan costs $15 per user per month and
adds shared folders, an admin console, and 180-day version history.

## File Size Limits

Individual files up to 10 GB can be synced on all plans. Files larger than
10 GB are skipped and reported in the activity log.

## Version History

Deleted files can be restored within the version history window of your plan:
30 days on Pro, 180 days on Team. The Free plan does not include version
history — deleted files cannot be recovered.

## Selective Sync

You can exclude folders from syncing on a per-device basis. Excluded folders
remain in the cloud and on your other devices but are removed from the local
disk of the device where they were excluded.

## Supported Platforms

Desktop clients are available for Windows 10+, macOS 12+, and Ubuntu 20.04+.
Mobile apps for iOS and Android support browsing and manual uploads but not
continuous background sync.
